ENGINEERED IN-HOUSE

From material to finished solution.

Tempsens combines engineering expertise with manufacturing capabilities across temperature sensing, heating, cables and thermal systems.

Engineering decisions made early can shape performance later. Keeping expertise and manufacturing capability close helps Tempsens develop solutions around demanding applications.
